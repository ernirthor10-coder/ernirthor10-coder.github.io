What is Metal Gear Solid?

Metal Gear is a stealth-action video game franchise created by Hideo Kojima and published by Konami. It debuted in 1987 on the MSX2 and popularized the "tactical espionage" genre, rewarding players for avoiding detection rather than fighting head-on. The series follows soldiers like Solid Snake and Big Boss as they confront giant bipedal nuclear-armed tanks called Metal Gears, along with shadowy conspiracies, genetic engineering, and artificial intelligence. Known for cinematic cutscenes, codec conversations, memorable bosses, and fourth-wall-breaking humor, it blends political commentary with anti-war themes. Landmark entries include Metal Gear Solid, Sons of Liberty and Snake Eater.

The creator of Metal Gear Solid

Hideo Kojima is the director and writer behind Metal Gear Solid. Born in Tokyo in 1963, he joined Konami in 1986 and created the original Metal Gear in 1987, a game centered on stealth rather than combat. In 1998, Metal Gear Solid brought that idea to the PlayStation with cinematic cutscenes, characters, and storytelling that explored nuclear weapons, war, and identity. Kojima's film-inspired style and willingness to break the fourth wall changed how games told stories. After leaving Konami in 2015, he founded Kojima Productions and released Death Stranding. Today he is regarded as one of gaming's most influential creators.

What studio created Metal Gear Solid?

Metal Gear Solid was developed and published by Konami, a Japanese company founded in 1969 in Osaka. It began as a jukebox rental and repair business before moving into arcade machines and home video games. Konami became known for franchises like Castlevania, Contra, and Silent Hill. The original Metal Gear Solid was made by Konami Computer Entertainment Japan and released in 1998 for the PlayStation, where it sold millions of copies worldwide. In 2005, Konami formed Kojima Productions as an internal studio to oversee the series. After tensions and Hideo Kojima's departure in 2015, Konami shifted its focus toward mobile games and pachinko machines.
